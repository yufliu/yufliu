# Intake Template

Copy, fill, send. All five required fields must be filled before drafting.

```
Restaurant:
City:
Email:
Items:
Stars:      (1-5)
```

## Follow-up block

Asked after intake, answered in whatever detail exists. "Nothing" is a valid
answer to any of them — unanswered sections get left out of the draft rather
than filled with invention.

```
Food     — beyond the dishes: portion, value, prep, texture, anything off?
Service  — pace, attentiveness, how a problem was handled, wait vs. quoted?
Shout-out— anyone or anything worth crediting by name?
Missing  — occasion, who came, first visit or regular, parking, noise, wait?
```

## Filled example

```
Restaurant: Kato
City:       Los Angeles
Email:      moninjas@gmail.com
Items:      milk bread with uni butter, dry-aged duck, shrimp toast
Stars:      5

Food     — the uni butter is the thing, way richer than expected. Duck was
           cooked perfectly, crispy skin. Shrimp toast was fine, not memorable.
Service  — tasting menu paced well, never felt rushed. About 2.5 hours.
Shout-out— our server Daniel walked us through every course without making it
           feel like a lecture.
Missing  — anniversary dinner, booked six weeks out. Street parking was rough.
```
